01 · Introduction

Better Nutrition Begins with Awareness

Understanding what we eat is an important step towards making informed decisions about our health and everyday lifestyle.

Food contributes to our energy, growth and general well-being. Learning about food groups, balanced meals, hydration, hygiene and physical activity can help people make informed everyday choices.

NutriCare is a proposed educational website that brings clear, practical health and nutrition information together with interactive learning and useful resources.

01

Nutrition knowledge

Understand food groups, nutrients and balanced meal choices.

02

Everyday wellness

Explore hydration, sleep, movement and hygiene practices.

02 · Problem Statement

Making Nutrition Awareness Easier to Access

Useful health information is most valuable when it is understandable, practical and easy to explore.

01

Scattered information

People may need to search several sources for guidance on food, hydration and healthy routines. NutriCare brings core learning topics into one organised place.

02

Complex explanations

Nutrition terms can feel difficult without familiar examples. Clear language and everyday food examples can make concepts easier to understand.

03

Limited interaction

Static materials may not let learners practise or check understanding. Quizzes with immediate explanations can support active learning.

04

Applying healthy habits

Knowing about healthy practices does not ensure they become routines. A simple tracker can help users record actions and reflect on them.

NutriCare addresses the need for an accessible, understandable and interactive platform for health and nutrition awareness.

03 · Objectives & Scope

What NutriCare Aims to Achieve

The project makes health education understandable, engaging and accessible through a practical website.

01

Improve nutrition awareness

Explain balanced diets, food groups, essential nutrients and sensible food choices.

02

Encourage healthy decisions

Share realistic guidance on hydration, activity, sleep, food safety and hygiene.

03

Support interactive learning

Offer a knowledge quiz with immediate feedback and clear explanations.

04

Build habit awareness

Let visitors record selected practices and reflect on daily progress.

PROJECT SCOPE

Educational website, not a medical service

The initial scope includes responsive learning pages, practical meal examples, a quiz, a basic habit tracker and educational resources. It does not diagnose conditions or replace advice from qualified health professionals.

04 · Website Features

Explore a Healthier Way to Learn

NutriCare brings nutrition education, practical health guidance and interactive learning into one website.

01

Learn about balanced diets, food groups and everyday ingredients such as fruits, vegetables, grains and pulses.

02

Daily health tips

05 · Technologies

The current project is a standalone website using HTML, CSS and JavaScript for a responsive, interactive learning experience.

01 · STRUCTURE

HTML

Organises semantic sections, headings, navigation, educational content and controls.

02 · PRESENTATION

CSS

Creates the visual design, page layouts, responsive styling and smooth transitions.

03 · INTERACTION

JavaScript

Supports presentation navigation, carousel motion and interactive website behaviour.

04 · VISUALS

Canvas & animation

06 · Expected Outcomes

Building a More Nutrition-Aware Community

NutriCare aims to make useful health and nutrition information easier to explore, understand and apply in everyday life.

01

Improved understanding

Learning pages and quiz feedback are intended to help visitors revisit nutrition and healthy-lifestyle topics.

02

Accessible resources

A topic-organised website can offer a convenient place to explore guides, tips and educational material.

03

Active learning

Quizzes and a simple habit tracker can encourage participation and personal reflection.

04

Community awareness

Clear information may help people consider practical choices within their own routines.
